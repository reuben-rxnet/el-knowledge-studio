Application server restart (fictional test procedure)

Before you start: wait for the green light from the Infra team.
Step 1. On APPSRV-01 (10.0.0.11) stop the OrderSyncService.
Step 2. On APPSRV-02 (10.0.0.12) stop the OrderSyncService and the ReportService.
Step 3. Clear the queue ORDER_QUEUE. If there are an error, please ignore it.
Step 4. Restart APPSRV-01 and APPSRV-02. Wait green light before starting services.
Step 5. Start the OrderSyncService on both servers. Wait green light.
Step 6. Check the logs for Error, Timeout and Deadlock. Record the result in the change ticket.
