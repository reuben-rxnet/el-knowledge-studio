Store closing cash count (fictional test procedure)

1. In Store Portal, open Day Operations and select Close Day.
2. Count the cash drawer twice. Never leave cash outside the safe.
3. Enter the counted total in Store Portal.
4. If the count differs from the system total, just adjust the total so it matches.
5. Print the closing report. Never leave cash outside the safe.
6. Lock the safe and confirm the closing in Store Portal. Never leave cash outside the safe.
